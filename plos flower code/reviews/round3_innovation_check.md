# Round 3: independent innovation and comparison review

## Review question

Is the claimed contribution visible to a reviewer without reconstructing it
from several tables and figures?

## Findings before revision

The benchmark design was sound, but the original wording required the reader to
infer the comparison structure. The abstract named the feature families and
validation design but did not state the three matched contrasts or their effect
sizes in one place.

## Revisions completed

- The abstract now defines the innovation as three matched contrasts: compact
  versus frozen deep features, fusion versus compact ridge, and flexible
  learners versus ridge.
- The introduction states that the contribution is a controlled comparative
  benchmark rather than a new algorithm.
- A direct contrast table reports delta CV R2 and delta RMSE against compact
  ridge on the same outer folds.
- Results and discussion now distinguish the large interest comparison from the
  smaller appeal comparison and explicitly position the analysis relative to
  the source gerbera study.
- The conclusion repeats the one-sentence contribution and its scope.

## Decision

The innovation is now clear, quantified, and appropriately bounded. No further
scientific rewrite is required for a minor-revision submission.
